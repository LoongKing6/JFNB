RESB
情绪与理性状态脑电数据集Rational and Emotional States Brainwave Dataset

RESB 面向情绪与理性认知的联合建模，记录被试在听觉刺激、数学计算和故事改编任务中的脑电活动，并以“情绪—理性轴”组织多种脑状态。

RESB supports joint modeling of emotion and rational cognition, recording EEG during auditory stimuli, mental calculation, and story adaptation tasks along an “Emotional–Rational Axis.”

01 · PARTICIPANTS

被试与伦理

32 名被试（5 名女性、27 名男性）来自湖北民族大学。研究经湖北民族大学伦理委员会及民族大学附属医院伦理委员会批准。

Thirty-two participants (5 female, 27 male) were recruited from Hubei Minzu University. The study was approved by the university and affiliated-hospital ethics committees.

02 · ACQUISITION

采集设备

使用 zekang eeg grade v48 设备，按国际 10–20 系统采集 35 通道 EEG，原始采样率 2 kHz，电极阻抗控制在 5 kΩ 以下。

EEG was recorded with a zekang eeg grade v48 system using 35 channels under the international 10–20 system at 2 kHz; electrode impedance was kept below 5 kΩ.

Raw recordings were acquired in BrainVision format. The manuscript specifies event-centered segmentation, resampling, filtering, resting-state regression denoising, and band analysis; file counts and size are not stated.

实验范式

一条连续的情绪—理性轴。

每次实验首先记录 2 分钟静息（neutral），随后依次完成四类情绪状态与两类认知状态任务。

Each experiment began with a two-minute resting-state baseline, followed by four emotional states and two cognitive states.

EMOTIONAL–RATIONAL AXIS
External engagement / internal withdrawal
Cognitive load / imagination
MEE · IEEemotional engagement
Neutral2-min resting state
Load · Imaginationrational cognition

情绪标签：MEE（轻度外向投入）、IEE（强烈外向投入）、MIW（轻度内向退缩）、IIW（强烈内向退缩）。理性状态：mental load 与 imagination。

Emotional labels: MEE, IEE, MIW, and IIW. Rational states: mental load and imagination.

PREPROCESSING

论文中的预处理链

基于瞬时标签划分 4 秒片段（标签前 2 秒 + 标签后 2 秒）；使用多相重采样统一至 500 Hz；按照任务相关脑区重新排列通道并形成局部脑区；进行 1–50 Hz 带通滤波；基于静息态 EEG 进行回归去噪。

Four-second segments were centered on instantaneous labels (2 s before and after). Polyphase resampling produced 500 Hz data; channels were reordered into task-related local regions, followed by 1–50 Hz bandpass filtering and resting-state regression denoising.
